Choose your audio device

  1. Connect the Fast Track Ultra to USB and turn on its power supply.
  2. Open System Settings → Sound and choose Fast Track Ultra as your output.
  3. Start with low monitor volume and play audio. This build operates at 48 kHz.

To remove the driver later, open the matching Fast Track Ultra uninstall package. Removal also briefly interrupts all audio.

If the device does not appear, restart your audio application. See the project's installation guide for diagnostics. Successful installation alone does not verify audio playback.
